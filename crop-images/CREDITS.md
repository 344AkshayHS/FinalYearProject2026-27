# Crop picture credits

Pictures are from Wikimedia Commons, Flickr, iNaturalist and other free-licence sites (found through Openverse); the
Source column names each one. Free to use under the licence shown. CC BY and CC BY-SA need the author and licence
named where a picture is shown, with a link to its page: the app shows this under each picture. The pictures are
copies saved at most 800 px on the longest side (JPEG), so they load fast in the app; nothing else in them is changed.

Every crop has up to 5 pictures in the same order: 1 field, 2 close-up, 3 pods (or ears / cob / fruit on the plant),
4 seeds (the harvested produce), 5 mature crop. A slot with no clear free picture is left empty. `crops.json` maps
each crop name, as the app writes it, to them. The app shows the harvested produce first.
